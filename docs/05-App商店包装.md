## Long Description

Error Flashcards helps students and exam learners turn scattered mistakes into study cards they can actually remember.

Capture a problem from a photo or paste text, extract the prompt, choices, answer, and explanation, then save everything into a clean flashcard flow. Instead of manually deciding what to review next, you can open the app and focus on the cards that matter most today.

The current version is designed for lightweight, offline-first study:

- Import problems from photos or pasted text
- Build structured flashcards with editable prompts, choices, and answers
- Review cards in a simple memory-based cycle
- Track weak spots by deck and tag
- Keep your learning data on device for fast, local access

Error Flashcards is a strong fit for:

- language learners
- certification candidates
- exam prep students
- anyone who solves lots of practice questions and wants better retention

## FAQ

### 1. Who is this app for?

It works well for students, language learners, certification candidates, and anyone doing repeated question-based study.

### 2. Do I need to organize everything manually?

No. You can import a problem from a photo or text first, then make quick edits before saving.

### 3. Does this version require an account?

No. The current version is designed for local, offline-first use.

### 4. Is this a subscription app?

No subscription messaging should appear in the current App Store version.

### 5. What makes it different from a normal mistake notebook?

A normal notebook stores mistakes. Error Flashcards helps you return to them on a review rhythm and identify the concepts you lose most often.
