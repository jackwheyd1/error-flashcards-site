Error Flashcards Support

Error Flashcards is an offline-first study tool for turning mistakes into reviewable flashcards and building a steadier review rhythm.

Getting Started

  • Open the Import tab and paste text or add a problem image from Photos or Camera.
  • Check the detected prompt, choices, and answer before saving the card.
  • Open Review to work through the cards that matter most today.

Troubleshooting

  • Recognition is inaccurate: use a clearer image with strong contrast and full question coverage.
  • No reminders arrived: make sure notifications are enabled for Error Flashcards in iPhone Settings.
  • A card seems missing: check deck filters and search terms in Library.

Data Notes

The current version stores study data locally on device for fast offline use and does not require account login for core study features.
